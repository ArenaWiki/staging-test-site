SlasherZA, the founder of the Arena Tower Defense Wiki. We wouldn't be here if not for his passion to make a wiki to share information about the game with his friends. 

Uchu, our dedicated staff member who worked hard to keep the site updated even during quiet times. 

Billie, Haemster & Casey, for their unwavering support and graciousness in working with us to make our sites the best resource for the community.
