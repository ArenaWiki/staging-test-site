Privacy & Terms

This site collects some analytics data using our analytics provider, Umami Analytics. All data is stored and collected on servers in the European Union region by Umami. We collect as little as possible: anonymous visit statistics through Umami, with no cookies included. The version of Umami that we are running is GDPR compliant. 

When you load a page, we record: 

  • The page you visited
  • The reffering website
  • User agent details (Device type, screen size, browser & OS) 
  • Language setting
  • Approximate location (Country/region/city)
  • Time of visit

Visits are grouped into anonymous sessions so we can count unique visitors, but these cannot be used to identify you. 

The site also sets no cookies. Your browser keeps two small items on your own device: your light or dark mode choice, and a cache of the wiki statistics (up to one hour, cleared when you close the tab) so they don't reload on every page. Neither is sent to us, and you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.
